Chongjian GE

I am a Research Scientist at Adobe Research, focused on scalable language-vision modeling. My research spans long-context modeling, agents, video world models, and image and video foundation models.

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from The University of Hong Kong in 2024, advised by Prof. Ping Luo. Previously, I was a visiting student at UC Berkeley with Prof. Masayoshi Tomizuka.

We are actively hiring self-motivated interns and seeking collaborations at Adobe Research. Feel free to reach out.
